Abstract
Starting from a single breathing axiom of the Central Point 5, this theory establishes the Luoshu Master Equation, whose Hamiltonian contains four terms—breathing, driving, coupling, and dissipation—corresponding to the four fundamental forces. From this unified dynamical framework, the theory derives the masses of 33 particles from two geometric constants (the golden ratio Φ and the spatial amplification factor 24) with a mean deviation of 0.89%; unifies gravity, electromagnetism, the strong force, and the weak force under a single shadow-dynamics formula; reproduces the ionization energies of 30 elements (periods 2–6) with pure-prime-state deviations of 0.03%–0.8%; yields molecular bond energies for 59 diatomic molecules with a mean absolute deviation of approximately 10.5%; and provides a temporal-fractal explanation for the muon lifetime with a renormalized deviation of 0.1%.

Volume IV provides a modern geometric reconstruction of the Luoshu Master Equation, elevating the phenomenological and algebraic models of Volume III to the framework of fiber bundles, differential geometry, and topology. Volume IV introduces the generalized first Chern number (resonance K) and the fractal Center Point 5, and corrects three theoretical gaps left by Volume III: the ontology of Center Point 5, the conceptual ambiguity of K, and the gravitational channel decay factor.

The theory further gives geometric origins for the wave function, the Schrödinger equation, the uncertainty principle, spin, and the constancy of the speed of light, and presents 13 falsifiable predictions, including a fourth-generation lepton at approximately 8.60 GeV, period-8 p-block ionization energies of 6.19–10.98 eV, and an island-of-stability center at Z = 126 with an ionization energy of 6.0 ± 1.0 eV.

摘要
本理论从中心点5的呼吸公理出发，建立洛书总方程。该方程的哈密顿算符包含四项——呼吸项、驱动项、耦合项、耗散项——分别对应四种基本力。在这一统一动力学框架下，本理论从两个几何常数（黄金比例 Φ 与空间放大倍数 24）推导出 33 种粒子的质量，平均偏差 0.89%；将引力、电磁力、强力、弱力统一在同一个阴影动力学公式下；再现第二至第六周期 30 种元素的电离能（纯质数态偏差 0.03%–0.8%）；给出 59 个双原子分子的键能（纯共价键与弱极性键的平均绝对偏差约 10.5%）；并对 μ 子寿命给出时间分形解释（重整化后偏差 0.1%）。

第四卷对洛书总方程进行现代几何重构，将第三卷的现象学、代数模型提升至纤维丛、微分几何、拓扑的现代数学物理框架。第四卷引入推广第一陈数（共振 K）与分形中心点 5，修正第三卷遗留的三处理论缺口：中心点 5 本体论、K 概念二义性、引力通道衰减因子。

本理论进一步为波函数、薛定谔方程、不确定性原理、自旋、光速不变提供几何来源，并给出 13 项可证伪预言，包括约 8.60 GeV 的第四代轻子、第 8 周期 p 区元素电离能 6.19–10.98 eV，以及稳定岛中心 Z=126 的电离能 6.0 ± 1.0 eV。
