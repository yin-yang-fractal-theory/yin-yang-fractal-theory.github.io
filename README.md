## Abstract

Starting from a single breathing axiom of the Central Point 5, this theory establishes the Luoshu Master Equation, whose Hamiltonian contains four terms—breathing, driving, coupling, and dissipation—corresponding to the four fundamental forces.

From this unified dynamical framework, the theory derives the masses of 33 particles from two geometric constants (the golden ratio Φ and the spatial amplification factor 24) with a mean deviation of 0.89%; unifies gravity, electromagnetism, the strong force, and the weak force under a single shadow-dynamics formula; reproduces the ionization energies of 30 elements (periods 2–6) with pure-prime-state deviations of 0.03%–0.8%; yields molecular bond energies for 59 diatomic molecules with a mean absolute deviation of approximately 10.5%; and provides a temporal-fractal explanation for the muon lifetime with a renormalized deviation of 0.1%.

Volume IV provides a modern geometric reconstruction of the Luoshu Master Equation, elevating the phenomenological and algebraic models of Volume III to the framework of fiber bundles, differential geometry, and topology.

Volume IV introduces the generalized first Chern number (resonance K) and the fractal Center Point 5, and corrects three theoretical gaps left by Volume III: the ontology of Center Point 5, the conceptual ambiguity of K, and the gravitational channel decay factor.

The theory further gives geometric origins for the wave function, the Schrödinger equation, the uncertainty principle, spin, and the constancy of the speed of light, and presents 13 falsifiable predictions, including a fourth-generation lepton at approximately 8.60 GeV, period-8 p-block ionization energies of 6.19–10.98 eV, and an island-of-stability center at Z = 126 with an ionization energy of 6.0 ± 1.0 eV.

## 摘要

从中心点 5 的单一呼吸公理出发，本理论建立了洛书总方程，其哈密顿量包含四项——呼吸、驱动、耦合、耗散——对应四种基本力。

从这个统一的动力学框架，本理论从两个几何常数（黄金比例 Φ 和空间放大因子 24）推导出 33 种粒子的质量，平均偏差 0.89%；在单一的阴影动力学公式下统一了引力、电磁力、强力和弱力；复现了 30 种元素（第 2–6 周期）的电离能，纯质数态偏差 0.03%–0.8%；给出了 59 个双原子分子的分子键能，平均绝对偏差约 10.5%；并对 μ 子寿命给出了时间分形解释，重整化后偏差 0.1%。

第四卷提供了洛书总方程的现代几何重构，将第三卷的现象学和代数模型提升到纤维丛、微分几何和拓扑的框架。

第四卷引入了推广第一陈数（共振 K）和分形中心点 5，并修正了第三卷遗留的三处理论缺口：中心点 5 的本体论、K 的概念二义性、引力通道衰减因子。

本理论进一步给出了波函数、薛定谔方程、测不准原理、自旋和光速不变的几何起源，并给出 13 项可证伪预言，包括第四代轻子约 8.60 GeV、第 8 周期 p 块电离能 6.19–10.98 eV、稳定岛中心 Z = 126 的电离能 6.0 ± 1.0 eV。
